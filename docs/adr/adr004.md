## 🏛️ ADR 004: Resonance Architecture vs. Statistical Memory (aNA AI Project v5.3b)

**Date:** April 13, 2026
**Status:** Validated
**Context:** In the current AI landscape, performance is often confused with storage capacity (memorizing billions of parameters). For the **aNA** project, we are exploring a different approach, modeled on human cognitive processes: **understanding through resonance**.
**Decision-maker:** Benoit Theriault

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer32x32.png)

### Problem

The goal is to move from a system that "recognizes" data to a system that "integrates" information by creating bridges between modalities (visual, auditory, haptic).

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

### Decision

We implement an active feedback loop where the success of a processing operation is not measured by simple matching, but by the stability of a cortico-thalamic resonance.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

#### Technical Principles

1. **Link Priority Over Storage**: aNA uses the `CORTICAL_RESONANCE_FACTOR` to keep the imprint of a previous stimulus active. This allows the "H" to be linked to the "e" to understand "He", rather than seeing two isolated events.

2. **Biological Modulation of Learning**: The use of the _`MYELIN_EFFICIENCY_COEFF`_ in _`neuromodulator.py`_ simulates brain plasticity. The more a connection is understood, the more efficient the "wiring" becomes, reducing processing latency.

3. **Interdisciplinary Inference**: By processing signal structures rather than raw data, the architecture allows the discovery of _"new avenues"_ for problem-solving by applying logic learned in one domain to a new context.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

#### Advantages

- **Cognitive Generalization**: The system becomes capable of handling the unknown by seeking resonances with the already known.

- **Metabolic Economy**: Once resonance (understanding) is established, the system requires less energy (optimized thalamic gain) to maintain attention.

- **Transparency**: The transition from "Unknown" to "Known!" at 80% in the logs reflects true neural integration, not a simple SQL query.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

#### Disadvantages

- **Learning Curve**: The system requires a stabilization phase _(a few cycles)_ for myelin and resonance to reach their maximum efficiency.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)
