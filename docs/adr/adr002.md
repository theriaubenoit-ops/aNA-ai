## 🏛️ ADR 002: Cerebellum Implementation as a Precision Corrector _(aNA AI Project v5.2b)_

**Date:** March 21, 2026
**Status:** Accepted
**Context:** Need to refine the motor and decisional outputs of Layer V to eliminate trajectory or logic errors.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer32x32.png)

### 1. The Problem (Uncertainty)

The signal coming from the cortex _(Layer V)_ is an intention, but it often lacks the necessary precision for flawless execution in a dynamic environment. Without a feedback loop, the system is prone to spatial and cognitive drift.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

### 2. The Decision (The Purkinje Brake)

We have integrated a Cerebellum module acting as a precision comparator.

- **Feedback Loop**: The Layer V signal is intercepted and compared to an _"internal model"_.

- **Selective Inhibition**: Uses Purkinje cell logic to _"sculpt"_ the signal by removing erroneous components.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

### 3. Justification (The Rigor)

- **Stability**: Ensures that the final action _(the red neon line in your diagram)_ is linear and stable, even in the presence of sensory noise.

- **Learning**: Allows the system to "sense" the error and adjust, a key step towards the awareness I've been seeking since I was 11.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

### 4. Consequences (Mastery)

- **Positive**: Sub-millimeter precision in simulations.

- **Challenge**: Requires perfect synchronization between the impulses of the thalamus and the deep nuclei of the cerebellum.
