## 🏛️ ADR 001: Transition to Hierarchical Cortical Architecture _(aNA AI Project v5.1b)_

**Date:** February 11, 2026
**Status:** Accepted
**Context:** Born from the evolution of the initial idea _(1979)_ to the functional "outline" version (JavaScript) _(v1.2 2025, assisted for the first time by AI)_ towards an increasingly complete biomimetic system _(v2-v4)_, but with a much more rigorous approach, thanks to scientific advances in neuroscience and new AI collaborations _(Python)_.
**Decision-maker:** Benoit Theriault

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer32x32.png)

### The Problem (The Shadow at the Origin)

Traditional AI architectures process information in a rigid _(fixed structure)_ and overly simplified way to be realistic _(2D, flat, and linear)_. To achieve true plasticity and energy efficiency, but also to reflect a "resonance of consciousness" more faithful to humans, we needed a structure capable of more accurately simulating its management and biological depth, as well as its metabolic cost of the nerve signal.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

### The Decision (Future Solution)

It was clear that we needed to begin by implementing a cortical structure with **6 interlaminar layers** _(L4 ⟶ L2/3 ⟶ L5 model)_ using **NumPy** as a high-precision vector engine _(> 90%)_ on the existing Neocortex of our first 3D spherical version (Fibonacci with x, y, z coordinates), which had already proven itself in our experimentation.

- **Layer IV (Input)**: Reception of the raw thalamic signal.

- **Layer II/III (Association)**: Integration and bridging to the Hippocampus _(Memory)_.

- **Layer V (Output)**: Motor command to the Cerebellum.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

### Justification (The Light)

- **Modularity**: Allows testing each layer independently (via `test_x.py`).

- **Efficiency**: The switch to vector graphics enables an instantaneous _"neon"_ flow, crucial for the future Dashboard.

- **Perspective**: This structure allows for an _"Ender"_-like change of reference frame, where the signal can be observed plunging from the sky _(Thalamus)_ to the earth _(Action)_.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

### Consequences (The Reflection)

- **Positive**: A _"Bulletproof"_ foundation for the next 10 years. Surgical clarity for any external observer.

- **Negative**: Higher initial complexity for new modules, requiring rigorous and systematic importing of _(`import numpy as np`)_.
