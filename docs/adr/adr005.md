## 🏛️ ADR 005: Multimodal Integration and Thalamic Gating (aNA AI Project v5.3b)

**Date:** April 15, 2026
**Status:** Validated
**Context:** Sensory Fusion and the "Wall" of Immutability
**Decision-maker:** Benoit Theriault

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer32x32.png)

### The Problem

In version 5.3b, we successfully implemented a **Thalamic Hub** capable of centralizing signals from three distinct sources: _(`input_haptic`, `input_visual`, `input_auditory`)_.

- The **Thalamic Hub** acts as a gatekeeper, filtering signals according to intensity and systemic gain (organism vitality).

- The **Fusion challenge** is the operation in _`main.py`_, grouping the data into a _`multimodal_input`_ dictionary, which is then transmitted to the cortical columns.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

### The Challenge

We encountered a structural limitation when transferring the signal to the **Hippocampus** _(`hippocampus.py`)_.

- **Observations**: The Hippocampus, in its current form, cannot index a multimodal dictionary _(error `TypeError: unhashable type: 'dict'`)_.

- **Scientific Limitation**: We are approaching the tipping point between the **raw digital signal** _(matrices, audio buffers)_ and the **symbolic representation** _(memory)_. Moving from the _"B Side"_ _(the biological)_ requires a dimensionality reduction method that we have not yet finalized.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

### Refinement Decisions (v5.3b)

- **Attentional Gating**: Maintain intensity filtering in the _`ThalamicHub`_ to simulate selective attention.

- **Philosophical Manifesto**: Include a footnote reminding users that this project is an interpretation and a _"digital bridge"_ to biological reality, without any claim to absolute accuracy.

![ ](/docs/assets/spacer16x16.png)

### Consequences

- **Positives**: Proof that centralized multimodal routing is viable _(at the input)_. Enhanced code resilience via security mechanisms _(`getattr`)_.

- **Negative**: The organism remains _"blind"_ to complex memories until the Hippocampus's hash function is rewritten _(to be done)_.
